Vision and Mission

Vision

To build an ideal generation with ethics, knowledge, skill, and human values, who will lead the nation and humanity by guiding their lives in the light of the Quran and Sunnah.

Mission

To build an ideal, leadership-oriented generation through education, character building, skill development, and humanitarian service in the light of the Quran and Sunnah.

Key Milestones
1981
A joint initiative of the Government of Bangladesh and WAMY held an International Youth Conference. President Ziaur Rahman allocated 7 acres of land.
November 2024
Official inauguration of the Wamy Mosque Complex and Development Center in the first building, according to the master plan.
30 May 2026
Delegates of Multaqa Mutatawwi'in fi Bangladesh visited the Wamy Center. The visit included a Qurbani program, school and madrasa tours, a science fair, and a lunch reception.
Future Plans
Preparations are underway to begin construction of the hospital and school. Work continues toward building the full complex according to the master plan.
