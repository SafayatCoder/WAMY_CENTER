Project Details

This modern mosque complex is one of the main projects of Wamy Center Bangladesh. Alongside fulfilling the religious needs of the local population, it will also host Islamic education and social awareness activities.

The mosque has been designed to ensure adequate natural light and ventilation. Its construction is being carried out following eco-friendly and sustainable building methods.

Sadaqah Jariyah — an ongoing charitable act whose reward continues endlessly. This mosque complex stands as a symbol of just such a lasting good deed.

Key Features

  • Spacious prayer hall
  • Modern ablution (wudu) and washroom facilities
  • Separate prayer area for women
  • Islamic library and research center
  • Quran education center
